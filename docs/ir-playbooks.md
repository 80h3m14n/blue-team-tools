# Incident response workflows


Incident response playbooks are structured, scenario-specific documents that provide security teams with step-by-step instructions to detect, contain, eradicate, and recover from cybersecurity incidents.


### IR playbooks components
- Objectives and scope
- Roles and responsibilities
- Incident categorization
- Communication protocols

To remain effective, playbooks should be regularly updated( ideally quarterly or after major incidents) and can be integrated with SOAR platforms to automate repetitive tasks like log analysis or network isolation.
